## Rotation logic
- Six fixed-length periods: 20-min periods at 9:40, 11:00 and 11:50; 30-min periods at 10:00, 11:20 and 12:10.
- The three 20-minute students (Hershell, Chance, Bailey) rotate through the three 20-min periods; the three 30-minute students (Luke, Emmanuel, Thomas) rotate through the three 30-min periods. Everyone shifts one period each week, so each student visits every period (early, middle, late) equally often (every 3 weeks) and never misses the same class period repeatedly.
- Harvey is fixed at 12:45-1:05 every week.

## Assumptions
- 10 weeks in Term 4, as in previous terms (Week 1 can be shifted to match the school calendar).
- Recess 10:30-11:00 has no lessons; 12:40-12:45 is a 5-minute changeover before Harvey's lunch lesson.
- Lesson lengths are exact: no overlaps, no gaps except recess and changeover.
- Name spelled "Hershell" as given in the request (earlier terms used "Herschell"/"Hershal").
