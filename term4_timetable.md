## Rotation logic
- Six fixed-length periods: 20-min periods at 9:40, 11:05 and 11:55; 30-min periods at 10:00, 11:25 and 12:15.
- The three 20-minute students (Herschell, Chance, Bailey) rotate through the three 20-min periods; the three 30-minute students (Luke, Emmanuel, Thomas) rotate through the three 30-min periods. Everyone shifts one period each week, so each student visits every period (early, middle, late) equally often (every 3 weeks) and never misses the same class period repeatedly.
- Harvey is fixed at 12:45-1:05 every week.

## Assumptions
- 10 weeks in Term 4, as in previous terms (Week 1 can be shifted to match the school calendar).
- Recess is 10:30-11:00; lessons resume at 11:05 (5-minute buffer after recess).
- Lesson lengths are exact: no overlaps, no gaps except recess.
